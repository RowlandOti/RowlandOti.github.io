Peer-Based Financial Insights — MSc Research

A prototype system for peer-based financial insights and recommendation in expense management.

Research question

How can expense patterns be grouped into meaningful peer segments to help people understand their spending behavior relative to comparable users?

Dataset and methodology

The research used an approximately 20,000-record personal finance dataset. Preprocessing included handling outliers and feature scaling, with attention to discretionary spending behavior.

Models and evaluation

K-Means and Gaussian Mixture Models were primary approaches, with DBSCAN and Spectral Clustering evaluated. Model assessment considered clustering quality measures including silhouette score, Davies–Bouldin index and within-cluster sum of squares.

Prototype

A Spring Boot backend supported the prototype for expense insights and recommendation. The work connected exploratory machine learning research with a practical application.

Research context

MSc Computational Intelligence · University of Nairobi
