Beattime Engine — Beat and Tempo Analysis

Music intelligence for editing decisions that follow rhythm, tempo and musical structure.

Purpose

Beattime Engine analyzes music to provide timing signals that Luminous can use for rhythmic cuts and other music-synchronized editing decisions.

Methods and tooling

The work includes beat and tempo analysis with Librosa and Essentia, with research into robust beat tracking and musically meaningful edit points.

Integration with Luminous

Audio timing informs edit planning alongside visual scene analysis and retiming. The goal is to make music synchronization part of a repeatable, programmable editing pipeline.

Engineering topics

Audio signal processing · Beat tracking · Tempo estimation · Automated video editing
