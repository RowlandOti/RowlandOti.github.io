Retime Engine — Video Retiming

A supporting engine for temporal effects and motion-aware retiming within the Luminous video editing workflow.

Purpose

Changing video speed while maintaining usable motion is an important part of short-form editing. Retime Engine isolates retiming capabilities from the higher-level editor so temporal effects can be orchestrated as part of a larger edit plan.

Integration with Luminous

Luminous coordinates retiming with beat-aware cuts, scene selection and FFmpeg-based rendering. The engine is a component of that pipeline rather than a separate end-user editing application.

Engineering topics

Video timing · Frame interpolation · Temporal effects · Media pipeline integration
